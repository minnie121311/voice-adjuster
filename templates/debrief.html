Debriefing

Understanding How Tone of Voice Agents Affects Users with Social Anxiety

Dear participant,

Thank you very much for taking part in this study. Your participation is highly appreciated!

We would like to provide you with some more details on the study. By taking part, you have valuably contributed to our understanding of how AI voice agent design (such as pitch and tone) is perceived differently by people with and without social anxiety, and how this understanding can improve the design of future voice-based digital tools.

Previous research suggests that people with social anxiety may process vocal tone and pitch differently from those without social anxiety. In this study, you listened to a series of voice recordings with different pitch levels and adjusted a voice's pitch to your own preference. We hypothesised that participants with elevated social anxiety would rate and prefer these voices differently from participants without social anxiety.

All the data collected will be kept strictly confidential. Data will be stored safely and used for academic research purposes only. Results may be presented at conferences and written up in journals. Results are normally presented in terms of groups of individuals; if any individual data were to be presented, it would be anonymous, with no means of identifying you.

During the experiment you were asked to reflect about yourself, which may cause negative emotions. If you feel like you need to talk to someone about your life and well-being, check out the resources below, or contact the project leader for further assistance.
