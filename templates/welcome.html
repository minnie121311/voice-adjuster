Understanding How Tone of Voice Agents Affects Users with Social Anxiety

What Is the Project's Purpose?

The aim of this project is to better understand how we need to design digital environments to improve users' mental health and wellbeing.

This study investigates how people perceive and respond to different voice qualities used by AI voice agents in workplace-style communication, and whether these responses vary depending on individual differences in social anxiety. You will complete a short questionnaire about social anxiety, listen to a series of short voice recordings and rate them, and then adjust a voice's pitch to the level you find most comfortable. You will also be asked some demographic questions (e.g., age, gender identity).

Why Have I Been Chosen?

You must be aged 19–35, currently based in the UK, a fluent English speaker, and have current or recent workplace experience (e.g., currently employed, actively job-seeking, or about to start a new role) to participate. This study is open to everyone regardless of whether you experience social anxiety or not — we are interested in the full range of experiences across all participants. Participants must be capable of providing informed consent and should not have photosensitive epilepsy or other conditions that would make it uncomfortable to complete an online questionnaire-based study.

Do I Have to Take Part?

It is entirely up to you whether to take part. If you decide to take part, you will be asked to complete a consent form. You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without it affecting any benefits you are entitled to. If you withdraw while the study is ongoing, you will be asked what you wish to happen to the data you have provided so far. Please note that once data collection is complete, your data will be anonymised and it will no longer be possible to delete it.

What Will You Do?

As part of the study, you will first complete a short questionnaire about social anxiety (the Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale). You will then complete two phases using your headphones:

  • Phase 1 — Voice Evaluation: listen to 36 short voice recordings (simulating workplace communication messages) and rate each on how trustworthy, anxiety-inducing, preferable, dominant, and warm it sounds.
  • Phase 2 — Pitch Preference: listen to a single neutral-toned voice sample and use an on-screen slider to adjust its pitch to the level you personally find most comfortable.

The study takes place entirely online through your web browser — no lab visit or special equipment is required beyond a computer or mobile device with headphones.

Will I Be Recorded?

This study does not involve any audio or video recording of you. The data collected are your questionnaire responses (including the social anxiety questionnaire), your ratings of the voice recordings, and your adjusted pitch preference, plus your Prolific ID (used only to verify your submission and arrange payment) and basic demographic information.

Please note:

  • Use headphones for the best audio quality, in a quiet environment
  • The study will ask about your mood and social anxiety, which may lead you to reflect on yourself and cause mild discomfort. You can pause or withdraw at any time without giving a reason
  • Your responses are recorded and stored securely and anonymously
  • You can withdraw at any time without penalty

What Are the Possible Risks and Benefits?

We are not aware of any significant risks or disadvantages to taking part beyond the mild discomfort described above; support resources (e.g., the Crisis Text Line) will be made available to you if needed. This study will advance our understanding of how voice agent design affects people with and without social anxiety, helping improve the design of future voice-based digital tools.

Compensation

You will be compensated via Prolific at the standard rate of £10 per 60 minutes, pro-rated to the maximum expected session length of 45 minutes (approximately £7.50 per participant). Payment is processed by Prolific once your submission is approved; if you complete the study in an implausibly short time, your submission may not be approved for payment.

Confidentiality and Data Privacy

All data collected will be anonymised and stored securely on password-protected, encrypted devices, in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR. Only the Principal Investigator and the Researcher on this project will have access to identifiable data (your Prolific ID). You will not be identifiable in any report or publication. Full details are provided on the Consent Form.
